# MOIRÉ — a 12" record

A self-initiated vinyl project. One idea, carried across the sleeve, the
reverse and the disc — and one piece of extremism that only exists while the
record is playing.

---

## Why this one

Your work already has a signature and you may not have noticed it: **the dot
screen**. It is on the home page, it is on the work page, it is what makes the
footage read as a surface instead of a picture. The op-art posters in Visual
Art are the same instinct — grids pushed until the eye gives up.

**Moiré** is the name for what happens when two of those grids meet at
slightly different angles: a third pattern appears that is in neither of them.
It is also, exactly, what happens in sound. Two tones a few hertz apart
produce a beat you can hear that neither tone contains.

So the record is about one thing: **interference**. The artwork is not a
picture of the idea. The artwork *is* the idea — two screens, and what happens
between them.

That is the minimal part. Here is the extreme part.

---

## The extremism

**The label is a strobe.**

Printed around the centre of the disc is a ring of fine radial bars, spaced so
that at exactly 33⅓ rpm under mains-frequency light they appear to stand still
— the same principle as the dots around a Technics platter.

Get the count wrong by one and the ring crawls. Get it right and it locks.

Which means:

- On the shelf it is a flat graphic ring.
- On the platter at the wrong speed it drifts.
- At the right speed it freezes.

**The artwork only completes itself when the record is playing at the correct
speed.** You cannot photograph that. You cannot put it on Instagram. It exists
only in the room, on a turntable, while the music is on.

That is the whole project in one move: a piece of graphic design that requires
the music to be playing in order to be finished.

*(The maths, so you can draw it: for 33⅓ rpm under 50 Hz light, use 180 bars.
Under 60 Hz, use 216. Draw them as a ring of even radial strokes, 50% duty —
bar as wide as the gap.)*

## The object

- **Artist:** FWA
- **Title:** MOIRÉ
- **Format:** 12", 33⅓ rpm
- **Release:** self-released
- **Catalogue:** FWA‑001

### Front

Almost nothing on it. The whole sleeve is **one dot** — a single halftone dot
blown up to 300 mm, cropped by the edges of the sleeve so you only ever see a
curve passing through. Flat black ink on uncoated cream stock.

No title on the front. No name. Nothing.

At that scale nobody reads it as a dot — they read it as a shape, a horizon, a
curve. It only becomes a dot once you have seen the back.

### Back

The same image at full resolution, so the picture resolves: a field of dots
at 65 lpi, 15°, and over it a second field at 75°. Where they cross, the
rosette appears.

Set small and tight in the lower left, in one weight, no bold:

Leave 40 × 25 mm clear in the lower right for a barcode.

### Disc labels

Both sides identical except for the side letter.

- The **strobe ring** at the outer edge of the label — fine radial bars, black
  on cream, nothing else in that band.
- Inside it, dead centre, only: **A** or **B**, set enormous, cut off by the
  spindle hole. The hole removes the middle of the letter. You read it anyway.
- Round the bottom, tiny: `FWA — MOIRÉ — FWA-001 — 33⅓ RPM`

No tracklist on the label. It is on the back; the label is for the strobe.

---

## Specs

- **Jacket:** 314 × 314 mm trim, 3 mm bleed, 5 mm safe
- **Label:** 100 mm diameter, 7.24 mm spindle hole, keep type 10 mm clear of
  the hole
- **Ink:** one colour, black, on uncoated cream. The whole thing is a screen —
  a second ink would be a different project
- **Screens:** 65 lpi for the back field. Any finer and the rosette disappears
  at arm's length, which is the distance it has to work at
- **Type:** one grotesque, two sizes, no bold. Track the small caps loose

## What to make

1. Front sleeve — the single dot
2. Back sleeve — the two screens and the rosette
3. Label A and label B, with the strobe ring drawn to the correct bar count
4. Printed inner sleeve — one screen only, no interference, so the inner is
   the "before" and the jacket is the "after"
5. **A 10-second video of the label spinning.** This is the hero asset for the
   site and the only way the strobe reads online — you already shoot and edit,
   so it is yours to make and nobody else's to copy
6. Digital artwork, 3000 × 3000 px
7. Three mockups: sleeve flat, disc on a platter, sleeve and disc together

---

## One warning

Do not add a second colour, a photograph, or a texture later because the
sleeve feels empty. It is supposed to feel empty. The restraint on the front
is what makes the back land, and the back is what makes the label land.

If it needs more, make the dot bigger.
